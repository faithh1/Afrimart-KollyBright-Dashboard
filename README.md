# Afrimart-KollyBright-Dashboard
An interactive Excel dashboard project developed to provide business insights into AfriMart's sales performance. The dashboard features KPI tracking, revenue trends, profit analysis, product performance metrics, and dynamic filtering using slicers for efficient decision-making.
---
## Business Questions Answered
- What is the total revenue, profit, and profit margin?
- Which countries contribute the most revenue?
- Which products generate the highest profit?
- What are the top-performing products by sales volume?
- How are revenue and sales trending over time?
- Which products and markets require management attention?
- How does business performance vary by year, country, and product category?
---
## Key KPIs
- Total Revenue: ₦10.45 Billion
- Total Profit: ₦2.10 Billion
- Total Units Sold: 1,017,885
- Profit Margin: 20%

## Key Insights

- Overall profitability stands at 20% profit margin, indicating that every ₦100 earned generates ₦20 in profit.
- Certain countries contribute significantly more revenue than others, highlighting key markets driving business performance.
= Product profitability varies considerably, with a small number of products contributing the largest share of profit.
